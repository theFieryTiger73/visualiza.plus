FAQ

( QUESTIONS )
What is a landing page?

A landing page is a single, focused page built for one offer and one action — like 'request a quote' or 'book now'. It has no menu or distractions.

How is it different from a full website?

A website presents your whole business. A landing page drives one decision. They work together: campaigns send traffic to the landing page.

Where do leads go?

Every form submission can go straight to your WhatsApp, email or CRM. You choose where you want to receive them.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. Landing pages are designed mobile-first, because most campaign traffic in Mozambique comes from phones.

Can I run ads to it?

Yes. Landing pages are built to be fast-loading and focused, which makes them an effective destination for Facebook, Instagram and Google ads.

Can I update the page later?

Yes. We can update text, offers and images for you, or connect the page to a system you manage.
