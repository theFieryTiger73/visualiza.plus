FAQ

( QUESTIONS )
Do I need a domain to have business email?

Yes. Business email runs on your own domain, like hello@yourcompany.com. If you do not have a domain yet, we can register one for you.

Can I move from Gmail or another provider?

Yes. We migrate your existing messages and contacts so nothing is lost, and your team keeps using the same familiar tools.

How many mailboxes can I have?

As many as your team needs — info@, sales@, accounts@, your name, or departments. We configure every mailbox individually.

Will my emails land in the inbox or spam?

We configure email authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) so your domain is verified. That protects your reputation and improves delivery.

Can I read my email on my phone?

Yes. We configure your email on Gmail, Outlook or your phone's mail app, so your team can reply from anywhere.
