FAQ

( QUESTIONS )
Why do I need a CRM?

Without a CRM, leads live in WhatsApp threads and memories. A CRM records every customer, conversation and follow-up in one structured system.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect WhatsApp so messages and leads can flow into your CRM automatically, instead of living only in your phone.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. You control access — each team member sees the contacts and pipelines relevant to them.

Can I see reports and dashboards?

Yes. Dashboards show your pipeline, open deals, follow-ups due and customer activity at a glance.

Can it be customised to my business?

Yes. We build custom business portals and workflows around how your business actually works.

What if I already have a messy list of contacts?

We clean and import your existing contacts, so you start with a structured database, not a blank page.
